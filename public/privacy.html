Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Mapped is a geography quiz run by Luke Hanna. You can play without an account. Signing in is only for saving games and appearing on the leaderboards.

If you sign in with Google

  • Mapped receives your Google account ID and email address, and nothing else from your Google account. They identify your account. Your email is shown only to you.
  • You pick a display name. It appears publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Games you play while signed in are saved: the setup, your result, and a log of your guesses and hints with their timing, used to check that leaderboard runs are genuine.
  • Best times this browser saved before accounts existed are added to your games when you first sign in. They are not ranked.
  • One cookie keeps you signed in. It holds a random token, and the server stores only a scrambled copy of it.

For everyone

  • When a game starts, the server stores a keyed hash of your IP address, never the address itself, to limit abuse.
  • Games played while signed out are kept for 90 days, so signing in later on the same browser adds them to your account. After that they are deleted.
  • Your theme and your personal best times are kept in your own browser. So are the claim tokens for games you play signed out, so they can be added to your account if you sign in, and a note, stored as a hash, of which accounts have already had this browser's old bests imported.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits without cookies or tracking.

What Mapped never does

No ads, no tracking across sites, and no selling or sharing your data. It is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site.

Deleting your data

Choose Delete account in the menu under your name. Your account, games and leaderboard places are removed straight away. Signing out removes the sign-in cookie from your browser.
